# Private Data Policy

This repository must not contain private recruitment email data.

The original model-development dataset contains private email content and is intentionally kept outside this repository.

Do not commit:

- real email bodies
- email subjects containing private information
- personal names
- email addresses
- recruiter information
- employer information derived from private emails
- application identifiers
- private URLs
- private training datasets
- private validation datasets
- derived BIO datasets containing private text

Public examples must be synthetic.

The trained model may be distributed separately from the private training data.
